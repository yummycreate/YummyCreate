These books are read by turning pages sideways, not by scrolling down. The number of pages on screen follows the size of your screen: one, two or three.

## Turning pages

On a phone, swipe sideways. On a computer, use the Previous and Next buttons under the book, or the left and right arrow keys.

At the end of a chapter, the button on the right becomes a link to the next chapter. Each chapter has its own URL, so you can send a single chapter to someone.
